    1. Make a fresh wallet for this position on Base and fund it with only the $200 you plan to use plus gas; keep the seed phrase offline and never reuse a wallet that holds other assets Cost: under $5 of gas for the first transaction. Time: 10 minutes.
    2. Allocate $200 of USDC into a Steakhouse Financial vault on Base, after reading the vault's stated strategy and its fee line, then record the transaction hash, the date and the amount in a note before you do anything else Cost: $200 of capital and about $10 of gas. Time: 20 minutes to set up.
    3. Size the ticket against the record, not against a template: $200 is 0.0000073% of its TVL on a protocol whose snapshot TVL is $2.75B, and the model implies $596M of value for the protocol, so the position is small enough to leave on its books through a drawdown and large enough that the protocol sees a real holding rather than a dust transaction Cost: $200, and the share of TVL above is what the protocol itself would see. Time: 15 minutes to size and double-check.
    4. Open the same $100 position on Ethereum as well, so Steakhouse Financial sees activity on both Base and Ethereum rather than one chain only; a protocol's TVL is counted per chain, so activity on both sides is what its own record actually shows Cost: $100 more of capital and about $3 of gas. Time: 15 minutes.
    5. Return every 7 days for one small qualifying action on Steakhouse Financial itself, a deposit, a withdrawal, a borrow, a swap or a governance vote, rather than a burst in one week Cost: about $2 to $4 of gas per visit. Time: 10 minutes a week.
    6. Hold the position at least 45 days so the balance is on the protocol's books across more than one weekly snapshot, and withdraw in two parts rather than all at once Cost: the $200 stays in the position for 45 days. Time: 45 days.
    7. Keep a farm log with the wallet address, every transaction hash, the dates and the exit plan, and check Steakhouse Financial's own site for the rewards it publishes: claim only from links on its own page and never pay a fee to claim Cost: nothing but the log and one deliberate check. Time: 10 minutes to write, 5 minutes a week to update.

    The same generator writes a plan for every ranked row: open any row and its detail view carries the identical seven-part plan, its own TVL, 7-day change and days without a token feeding the sizing and cadence. Nothing above is hand-written: the seven items are the strings main.ts wrote into board.json for rank 1, quoted unedited so the text read and the data rendered are the same words.
